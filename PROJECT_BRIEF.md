# Section Furniture — digital showroom brief

## Business context

Section Furniture is a New Cairo furniture design and manufacturing business founded in 2019. It operates a factory in Moasset El Zakaa / Al-Zakah Foundation area and a showroom in Silver Star Downtown Mall, Fifth Settlement. The offer includes interior work, custom woodwork, furniture manufacturing, cladding, beds, kitchens and dressing rooms.

The existing Strikingly site contains real project imagery and a useful design → prototype → build story, but the information architecture is essentially one very long gallery. It does not clearly separate project inquiries from product shopping.

## Product strategy

Position the new experience as a **premium digital showroom with two conversion paths**:

1. **Start a project** — interiors, kitchens, dressing rooms, cladding, bespoke furniture and execution.
2. **Shop the collection** — a tightly edited group of standard or controlled made-to-order pieces.

The service journey remains primary if custom projects produce most of the revenue. The shop supports the brand and creates a faster entry point.

## Recommended launch structure

- Home
- Services
- Projects / project detail
- Shop / product detail
- Process
- About / factory / showroom
- Consultation
- Contact

## Commerce rules

- Use direct purchase for fixed-price products with clear dimensions, material, lead time and delivery.
- Use controlled made-to-order for a limited set of sizes and finishes with a clear lead time or deposit.
- Use quotation for kitchens, dressing rooms, built-ins, cladding, full interiors and any work dependent on site measurement or installation.
- WhatsApp is a secondary support channel, not the primary structure for every action.

## Recommended art direction

Warm editorial restraint inspired by the operating model of Soho Home, without copying its brand identity. Large photography, calm typography, tactile materials, disciplined spacing and a single conversion accent.

Palette:

- Ink — `#1C1B18`
- Chalk — `#F3EEE6`
- Walnut — `#6B4B37`
- Oxide — `#B45D3C`
- Sage — `#74755D`
- Brass — `#C7A46A`

## Content required before implementation

- Primary business goal and revenue priority
- First 6–12 products, prices and availability model
- Dimensions, materials, finishes, lead times and delivery rules
- 4–6 best completed projects with location, scope and materials
- One strong hero room shoot plus workshop/process photography
- Testimonials, partner logos and warranty/after-sales information
- Inquiry owner and product/content owner after launch

## Implementation approach

Use the official OpenAI frontend skill for art direction and interface quality, a reusable component/design system for the website, and browser verification for desktop/mobile flows. Select a commerce platform only after catalogue size, inventory operations, payment method and delivery rules are confirmed.
